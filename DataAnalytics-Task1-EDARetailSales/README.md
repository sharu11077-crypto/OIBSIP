# DataAnalytics-Task1-EDA-RetailSales.ipynb
# Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Retail Sales Data

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, and revenue patterns.

The datasets were combined using common identifiers such as customer ID, product ID, store ID, and date to create a comprehensive dataset for analysis.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyse monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Understand customer age-group distribution
- Analyse customer gender distribution
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products
- Analyse revenue generated by product category
- Explore relationships between numerical variables using a correlation heatmap
- Generate business insights and recommendations

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the following datasets:

- `sales.csv`
- `customers.csv`
- `products.csv`
- `stores.csv`
- `calendar.csv`

The datasets were merged to create a final dataset containing **1,000,000 rows and 30 columns**.

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Monthly and Quarterly Revenue Trends

Sales revenue was analysed over time to understand monthly and quarterly performance.

### 2. Customer Age Group Analysis

Customers were grouped into different age categories to understand the largest customer segments.

### 3. Gender Distribution

The customer base was analysed based on gender.

### 4. Top 10 Best-Selling Products

Products were ranked based on the total quantity sold.

### 5. Revenue by Product Category

Total revenue was calculated for each product category.

### 6. Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created to understand relationships between numerical variables such as age, quantity, unit price, discount, revenue, cost, and profit.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Total revenue generated was approximately **25.49 million**.
- Total profit generated was approximately **10.19 million**.
- **Dark Chocolate 50%** was the best-selling product with **208,996 units sold**.
- **Praline** was the highest revenue-generating category, generating approximately **6.67 million**.
- The **55–64 age group** had the highest number of customers.
- The customer base was almost evenly distributed between male and female customers.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on promoting the best-selling products to maintain strong sales performance.
- Prioritise the Praline and White product categories because they generated the highest revenue.
- Target customers aged 35–64 with suitable marketing campaigns.
- Use monthly and quarterly sales trends to improve inventory planning and promotional strategies.
